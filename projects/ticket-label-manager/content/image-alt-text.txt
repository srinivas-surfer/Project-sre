Slide 1: The engineering story. Ticket Label Governance. A Flask application that retrieves Jira issues, evaluates conditional label requirements, and supports individual and selected-ticket updates.
Slide 2: Why I implemented it. The operational problem. The review task is distributed across individual issue pages and a separate understanding of policy. Parent/child and closure conditions must be remembered while checking each ticket.
Slide 3: How it works. From input to outcome. Choose the review scope → Retrieve and normalize issues → Evaluate active policy groups
Slide 4: What changed. One review workflow. Rule evaluation, visible gaps, and selected-ticket updates.

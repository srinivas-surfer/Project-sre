Slide 1: The engineering story. Cloud Portfolio Platform. A resume-documented containerized portfolio with independently delivered services, explicit release approvals, rollback history, and automated operational reporting.
Slide 2: Why I implemented it. The operational problem. The operating problem is how to make a small web platform repeatably deployable and recoverable, rather than relying on an undocumented change process or a mutable image tag.
Slide 3: How it works. From input to outcome. Resolve and deliver public traffic → Serve independently packaged components → Build and approve a release
Slide 4: What changed. N-1 rollback readiness. Previous verified images and per-service release history.

Observability Command Center

The command center addresses two connected problems: fragmented operational context and the reliability of the collection workflow itself. A dashboard is less useful if its collectors block each other, duplicate work, or fail without making the failure visible.

The implementation:
• Register and select the environment
• Schedule bounded collection cycles
• Claim and execute jobs
• Evaluate and persist monitoring evidence
• Correlate and inspect

What changed:
A shared control plane and UI connect collection state, service observations, baselines, and correlation evidence.

Case study / Independently prepared public edition

Observability Command Center

Explore a fictional service landscape, switch incident scenarios, and inspect the evidence behind a possible shared cause.

The problem

Operators need a concise way to relate user symptoms to dependency changes without confusing coincidence with causation.

The implementation choice

Show timestamps, affected services, and a hypothesis together while making the evidence limits explicit.

A static scenario model drives the topology, metrics, and event inspector. All scenario records are part of the public demo source.

What you can verify

  • Normal, dependency, and recovery scenarios
  • Per-service signal cards
  • Event filtering and inspection
  • Explicit correlation limitations

Switch to dependency slowdown, filter to checkout, and inspect the shared database signal. Then compare the recovery scenario.

Scope and limits

Signals and timelines are scripted teaching examples, not live telemetry. Correlation is an illustrative hypothesis, not an automated root-cause finding. No operational actions are available.

This public edition demonstrates engineering concepts. It makes no claim about measured client impact or production readiness.

03 / My foundation

Cloud & DevOps roots.
An SRE
way of thinking.

Read my full resume ↗

My strength is connecting infrastructure, application behavior, and operational needs to understand how a system stays reliable.

I started in infrastructure and cloud operations, then moved through observability and DevOps into SRE. At Avasoft, I helped establish an SRE practice and worked as an Observability SME across client environments, evaluating tools and shaping monitoring around different workloads.

In my SRE role, that foundation extends to production incidents, Kubernetes operations, platform migration, release validation, and performance planning. I approach solutions by understanding dependencies and failure modes, choosing useful signals, and planning how teams will respond and recover.

Automation is one part of that work. I use AI-assisted implementation to develop practical tools where repeated tasks or missing visibility call for a better workflow.

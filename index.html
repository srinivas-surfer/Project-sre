01 / How I approach a problem

Start with the system.
Find the repeated work.

Reducing toil is a core part of SRE: taking repetitive operational work and designing a better way to handle it.

01

Understand the system

Map the services, dependencies, data, and failure paths. What does healthy behavior look like, and what does the team need to know?

02

Find the repetition

Look for recurring checks, queries, reports, and updates. Which steps follow clear rules, and which still need an engineer’s judgment?

03

Design the workflow

Define inputs, decision points, outputs, and failure handling. Keep the result useful for the person who will investigate or take action.

04

Implement with AI

Use AI to help turn the design into code. Review the behavior against the original problem, including exceptions and the need for human oversight.

The goal: less time repeating the same steps, more time understanding and improving the system.
